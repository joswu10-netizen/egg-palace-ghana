OUR BUSINESS

About Egg Palace Ghana

Egg Palace Ghana supplies fresh eggs and live chickens.

Our egg selection includes eight categories, from Under Grade to Jumbo. We also supply white and brown chickens.

Customers can compare prices, send WhatsApp enquiries, arrange farm pickup or ask about delivery. Our farm is located in Teacher Mante, Eastern Region, Ghana.

CUSTOMER SUPPORT

Frequently Asked Questions

How many eggs are in a crate?

Every crate contains 30 eggs.

How many egg categories do you sell?

We offer Under Grade, No Grade, Pullet, Small, Medium, Large, Extra Large and Jumbo.

Do you sell live chickens?

Yes. We offer white and brown chickens, subject to availability.

Where is Egg Palace Ghana located?

Teacher Mante, Eastern Region, Ghana.

How can I place an order?

Use our Quick WhatsApp Order Enquiry form or call 024 426 9337 or 055 372 8622.

Can I collect my order at the farm?

Yes. Please contact us to arrange collection in advance.

Do you offer delivery?

Contact us to enquire about delivery to your location. Availability must be confirmed.

How much does delivery cost?

Delivery fees depend on your location and order details.

Are the listed prices final?

Contact our team to confirm current prices and stock before ordering.

Does the website accept payment?

No. The website prepares a WhatsApp or email enquiry. Payment arrangements must be discussed directly with our team.
