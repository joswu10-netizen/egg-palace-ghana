OUR BUSINESS

About Egg Palace Ghana

Egg Palace Ghana supplies fresh eggs and live chickens from Teacher Mante, Eastern Region, Ghana.

We offer eight egg categories, from Under Grade to Jumbo, alongside white and brown chickens.

Our website makes it easier to explore our products, view our farm facilities, compare prices and send order enquiries through WhatsApp.

CUSTOMER SUPPORT

Frequently Asked Questions

How many eggs are in one crate?

Each crate contains 30 eggs.

How many egg categories do you sell?

Eight categories: Under Grade, No Grade, Pullet, Small, Medium, Large, Extra Large and Jumbo.

Do you sell live chickens?

Yes. We offer white and brown chickens, subject to availability.

Where is Egg Palace Ghana located?

Teacher Mante, Eastern Region, Ghana.

How can I place an order?

Complete our Quick WhatsApp Order Enquiry form, or contact us directly using the phone numbers provided.

Can I collect my order from the farm?

Yes. Please arrange collection in advance.

Do you offer delivery?

Contact our team to enquire about delivery to your location. Delivery availability must be confirmed.

How much does delivery cost?

Delivery charges depend on your location and order details.

Are the listed prices final?

Please confirm current product prices and availability before completing your order.

Can I pay through the website?

No. The website prepares WhatsApp and email enquiries only. Payment arrangements must be confirmed directly with our team.
